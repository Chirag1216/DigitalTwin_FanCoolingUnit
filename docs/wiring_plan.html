Fan cooling unit · digital twin hardware

Fan Twin Wiring Plan

ESP32 DevKitC driving an Arctic P12 Pro fan, with an MPU6050 for vibration and a DS18B20 for enclosure temperature. Pins match firmware/fan_rpm/config.h. The INA219 is optional and currently removed.

GNDEvery ground is joinedThe ESP32, the 12 V supply's minus and every module share one GND rail. Without it the PWM and tach signals have no reference.
12 V12 V only touches the fanThe supply's plus goes to the fan's yellow wire and nowhere else. One slip onto an ESP32 pin destroys it.
3V3Sensors and pull-ups use 3V3MPU6050, DS18B20 and every pull-up resistor connect to the ESP32's 3V3 pin. Never 5 V, never 12 V.

Before and after power-on

Before plugging in

  • Header pins are soldered to the MPU6050 board, not just pushed through.
  • Multimeter in beep mode across the 3V3 and GND rails: it must not beep.
  • The 12 V plus wire reaches the fan's yellow wire and nothing else.
  • On breadboards with split power rails, both halves are bridged.
  • Plug in USB first, then switch on the 12 V supply.

Serial Monitor at 115200 should show

# preset 0 (OFF) -> duty 0%
# INA219 not found - power columns will be nan (continuing)   ← fine while it's removed
# Calibrating - keep everything still...
# Bias ax=… ay=… az=…
# Ready. Presets: p 0=OFF p 1=LOW …
# MQTT connected

If you see # MPU not found, the firmware stops there and never starts Wi-Fi. Upload firmware/i2c_check and its verdict will tell you which wire is wrong.

Adding the INA219 back later

The INA219 shares the I²C bus with the MPU6050 and sits in the fan's 12 V wire to measure its current.

INA219 pinToNote
VCC / GND3V3 rail / GND railLogic power for the chip.
SCL / SDAGPIO22 / GPIO21Same lines as the MPU6050. Give it its own wires to the ESP32 pins so that removing it can't disconnect the MPU.
VIN+12 V supply +Replaces the direct supply-to-fan wire.
VIN−Fan yellow (+12V)All fan current flows through the INA219 shunt, so solder or screw these two. Loose jumpers here caused the earlier problems.
